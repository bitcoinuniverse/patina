A Bitcoin artifact

It deepens for every block it does not move.

Depth is not a score anyone hands out. It is the distance between two block heights, and any Bitcoin node can compute it.

  • What this is

    PATINA marks one Bitcoin output as the carrier of an artifact. Depth is the number of blocks that output has stayed unspent. Spend it and depth returns to zero. The stretch that just ended is engraved as a ring and stays in the record forever.

  • What you can own

    A Firstlight Seal, one of the founding cohort. They can only be claimed inside a single window of 4032 blocks, about four weeks at ten minutes per block. When that window closes, no new Firstlight Seal can ever exist.

  • What it costs

    No price. You pay Bitcoin network fees, and you seal at least 100 000 sats of your own bitcoin into the artifact as its endowment. That bitcoin stays yours. You can spend it whenever you like, and doing so resets the depth to zero.

Say it before anyone has to ask

What depth proves, and what it does not

It proves

The outpoint has not moved. Anyone can check that against a Bitcoin node with no trust in us at all. Pair it with a signed attestation over the artifact id and a recent block hash, and you also know the key is alive.

It does not prove

That the same person held it the whole time. Keys can be sold off chain, and the chain cannot see that. Depth is a fact about an output, not a biography of a person.

Two more things we would rather say ourselves than have you discover.

  • Founding membership can be farmed. Anyone willing to park real bitcoin can take as many Firstlight Seals as they can fund. There is no cap and no per person limit, because the protocol has no idea what a person is. Concentration figures are published live during the window rather than after it.
  • Buying an artifact moves it. A transfer spends the carrier, so the depth resets to zero and the stretch you paid for closes as a ring. You inherit the record, not the depth.

The full risk list is a page of its own, not a footnote.

Reset on move is not a rule we enforce

It is inherited. Spending an output destroys it, and a destroyed output has no age. There is no admin who can decide your depth survived a transfer, because there is nothing left to decide about.

That is the whole trick. The scarce thing is not a token supply. It is the decision not to touch something.

A ring is what a stretch leaves behind

When a carrier is spent, the stretch closes and the record gains a ring: the height it started, the height it ended, its depth, and the value it carried. Rings accumulate. They are the part nobody can reset.

If the spend has an eligible successor output, the artifact continues on that output at depth zero. If it does not, the artifact becomes a relic and stops moving forever.

The genesis asset

Firstlight Seals

One cohort. One window of 4032 blocks. Zero team allocation, zero reserve, zero price, no supply cap.

Claiming uses an aged commit and reveal. Your commit output has to be at least 144 blocks old before you can reveal it, and only you can spend it. That is why a claim cannot be sniped: watching the mempool tells a sniper nothing they can act on.

The supply is whatever the window produced. We are not going to pretend that number was designed.
