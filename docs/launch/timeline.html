Timeline

Measured in blocks, because blocks are what the protocol reads. Days are estimates at ten minutes per block and nothing depends on them.

Heights, not dates

Every milestone below is relative to h_open, the height the founding window opens. Publish heights and let people convert. A published date will drift and then look like a broken promise.

Before the window

Preparation, with nominal lead times.
WhenWhat happens
About 2016 blocks before open, roughly 14 days Announce. Publish h_open, h_close, grace_end, spec_sha256, this documentation, and the golden vectors. State the limits in the announcement itself.
About 1008 blocks before, roughly 7 days Publish the signet rehearsal results and the state root agreement between two implementations. Anyone who wants to build their own indexer now has a week and a target.
About 288 blocks before, roughly 2 days Publish a plain reminder that a commit must age 144 blocks before its reveal, so the practical deadline for committing is a day earlier than people assume.
Final block before open Confirm both indexers are synced, alerts are live, and the on call rota starts.

The window

From open to close, 4032 blocks, nominally 28 days.
HeightMilestoneWhat to do
h_open Founding commits are accepted Nothing is created yet. The first valid reveal cannot land before h_open + 144.
h_open + 144 The earliest possible founding artifact Watch for the first reveals and for a spike in SEED_COMMIT_TOO_YOUNG, which means people are miscounting.
h_open + 1008 Roughly one week in Publish the first concentration statistics from live data. Not a celebration post, a measurement.
h_open + 2016 Halfway, one difficulty period Publish the first census epoch summary. Re-run cross indexer verification.
h_close - 288 Two days of commits left Remind people that a commit landing now still needs 144 blocks before the reveal.
h_close - 144 The last commits that can be revealed comfortably State plainly that commits after this point are increasingly tight, and that h_close is exclusive.
h_close Founding commits stop being accepted A commit confirming at exactly h_close is outside the window. Say so before, not after.

The grace period

4032 blocks after close, nominally 28 more days.
HeightMilestone
h_close to grace_endReveals of commits made inside the window still produce founding artifacts
grace_endThe last height at which a reveal can be founding. This is h_close + 4032, inclusive.
After grace_endThe founding cohort is closed permanently. Seeding continues with the 10000 sat minimum and founding: false.
Nothing reopens it

There is no extension, no second window, and no exception. Anyone who missed it minted a non founding artifact, which is a real artifact with the same rules for depth and rings.

Operational rhythm during the window.

What is deliberately absent

  • No launch countdown timer in hours and minutes. The countdown is a block count or it does not exist.
  • No milestone celebrations tied to mint counts. A count is a measurement, not an achievement.
  • No scarcity messaging as the window closes. The window closing is a fact and it needs no adjectives.
  • No influencer allocations, no early access, no allowlist. There is nothing to allocate.

After the window

Operations continue unchanged. Depth keeps accumulating, rings keep being engraved, and the census keeps reporting per epoch. The interesting part of PATINA starts after the launch is over, which is the point of building it this way.
